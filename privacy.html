Privacy Policy

Hermes Connection (a personal-use app). Last updated: November 2026.

What the app accesses

With the owner's explicit consent, the app requests read-only access to the owner's own Google account: Gmail (gmail.readonly) and Google Calendar (calendar.readonly). It cannot send, modify, or delete email or calendar events.

How the data is used

The data is used only to produce summaries and answer questions for the account owner, such as listing today's calendar events and highlighting important unread email.

Storage and sharing

The app's access token is stored on the owner's own computer. We do not sell, rent, or share Google user data with any third party, and we do not use it for advertising. Content that the owner asks the assistant to process may be sent to the AI service the owner has chosen in order to generate a response, and is used only for that purpose.

Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the stored token from their computer.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
